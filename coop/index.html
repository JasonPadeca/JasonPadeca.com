Koinonia Homeschool Group

Providing elective and enrichment opportunities for Christian homeschoolers in the Phoenix area.

What is Koinonia?

Koinonia is a transliterated form of the Greek word κοινωνία, which refers to concepts such as fellowship, joint participation, and the share which one has in anything.

We started as three homeschool moms in Phoenix, Arizona with a vision for a Christ-centered homeschool community for our kids and ourselves. We each come from different backgrounds and paths in life, but we’ve been brought together by our love for Jesus Christ and our passion for our families and our kids’ education. We believe that a big part of their education is teaching them to participate in the body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12: 12-31) and the community in which we live (Jeremiah 29:7). We also want to make the most of our different gifts and talents, and recognize our own need as moms to have community and be encouraged by one another.
