Class Descriptions – Fall 2026

Class sign ups will be open 8/6 – 8/13.

In addition to the specific class materials that some classes require, Koinonia Homeschool group recommends that each student bring the following items each week:

  • Personal Backpack
  • Personal Lunchbox (for lunch and/or snacks)
  • Reusable Water Bottle
  • Binder with dividers (to keep all papers from class)

–Session 1-

Upcycled Clothes

Teachers: Sharon Rodriguez and Azita Malaki

Assistant: Marlowe Garay

Class Description: A fun class about taking existing pieces of clothing and creating fun new pieces that make a fashion statement!

Ages: 12+

List of Materials: Clothes to upcycle!

Materials Fee: None

Prerequisite: I need them to have taken the introductory sewing class I have offered before OR make an appointment to show me their skill level. This class will be intentionally challenging.

Class Limit: 8

Homework Level: Light

Woodburning – Beginner

Teacher: Kashmira Donahey

Assistant: Ashli Brostrom

Class Description: Students will learn the art of woodburning (pyrography) by safely using a woodburning tool to create decorative artwork on wood. Throughout the course, students will practice basic strokes, shading, lettering, textures, and design transfer techniques while completing several mini projects that build confidence and creativity.

Ages: 13+

List of Materials:

1. Wood burner kit with stand

2. Sandpaper 220 grit
3. UNTREATED Scrap wood or wood from a hobby store for weekly classes (do not get plywood, MDF, or particle board- they release toxins when burning)
4. Pencil
5. Sand eraser
6. Small needle nose pliers to change tips when they’re hot
7. Heat resistant silicone mat similar to this one

Materials Fee:$10 (transfer paper, wood for final project/showcase, other materials to woodburn)

Prerequisite: None

Class Limit: 8-10

Homework Level: None

Grimm’s Tales

Teacher: Valarie Denny

Assistant: Katie Lanting

Class Description: Together we will explore the classic tales of the Brothers Grimm. We will read some of the stories together, examine the parts of a good fairy tale, and practice writing our own through games and activities, both as individuals and in groups. Prepare to meet Rumplestiltskin, Snow White, the Goose Girl, and many others you may never have heard of before.

Note: The original Grimm’s tales are a little more gruesome than their modern versions tend to be. A list of the stories we will be covering is available by request. Students who are especially sensitive may prefer to take a different class.

Ages: 9-12

List of Materials: A notebook and pencil.

Materials Fee: None

Prerequisite: Students should be comfortable hand writing a paragraph of 3-5 sentences on their own.

Class Limit: 8

Homework Level: Light

Recorder Karate

List of Materials: (2) 3-Ring Binders or 3-Prong Folders for music (1 for home and 1 for Koinonia)
(1) Recorder for home (optional but highly recommended for practice at home) A 2 or 3 piece recorder is best. Here’s an option that could work well.

Wonder- Book Club

Teacher: Denise Caswell

Assistant: Michelle Baldwin

Class Description: Join Auggie as he begins his first year of (public) school in 5th grade! He wasn’t able to attend school due to medical reasons but now he will meet an entire classroom full of possible friends. Experience a year of opportunities with Auggie and his family. Be ready to share your thoughts as we will take turns reading aloud in class every week.

Further information: The theme of this book is “choose kind”, but RJ Palacio is not a Christian author and therefore her standards and conclusions are not Biblically based. A variety of topics will be presented, such as: bullying, death of family member, afterlife concepts including reincarnation, suicide, swearing (“Oh God”), dishonesty, death of family pet, teenage romance, divorce and remarriage, modesty, physical fighting, genetic counseling and abortion, and pagan ideas of fortune and self knowledge. At appropriate points we will discuss the Christian worldview and counter false ideas with scripture. Some of these topics are lightly touched upon while others are presented multiple times. If you have any questions please let me know and I can go over any details of concern.
In order to complete this book, the readings have been broken down into 12 sections which are roughly 26 pages a week. I can provide the reading schedule if you’d like to get a head start on the book. We will read in class and whatever is not finished will need to be read at home. During the 2-week fall break reading will be required in order to finish by November.

Ages: 9-12

List of Materials: Wonder by R. J. Palacio (E-book or physical book)

Materials Fee: Wonder Discussion Questions – Teachers Pay Teachers $5.50

Prerequisite: motivated independent reader, reading aloud fluency

Class Limit: None

Homework Level:  weekly reading required

Lego City Builders

Teacher: Katie Griffith

Assistant: Leah Morales

Class Description:

Get ready to build, create, and explore! In this hands-on LEGO City Builders class, kids will use their imagination and building skills to create their own bustling LEGO city. Students will design and build houses, businesses, roads, vehicles, parks, and other exciting city features while learning how individual creations can come together to form one big community.

Along the way, we’ll explore how real cities are planned and how they function. Students will learn about the different parts of a city, how roads and neighborhoods are organized, why cities need services like police, fire departments, schools, and hospitals, and how people work together to keep a community running. We’ll discuss how city planners make decisions about where buildings, parks, businesses, and roads should go—and then put those ideas into practice through our own LEGO city!

Each class will include a new building challenge or city project, encouraging creativity, problem-solving, teamwork, and independent thinking. Whether your child is an experienced LEGO builder or just loves to create, they’ll have fun bringing their ideas to life while discovering what makes a city a thriving community—one brick at a time!

**Skills:** Creativity, engineering, problem-solving, spatial reasoning, teamwork, and an introduction to community planning

Ages: 5-9

List of Materials: Small Lego set (if possible)
Extra Lego pieces/sets no longer needed

Materials Fee: None

Prerequisite: None

Class Limit: 10

Homework Level: None
