Drafted By Dice

Gatestormers

A fantasy novel I don't get to fully control. At every turning point I set the stakes, roll real dice, and write whatever comes up.

How it works

  1. Stakes first Before each roll, I write down what a failure, a success and anything in between would mean for the story.
  2. Then the roll Real dice, under the standard fifth-edition rules. I don't reroll and I don't fudge.
  3. Then the story Underlined passages ⚄ were decided by a roll. Hover over one (or tap it on your phone) to see the roll, what was riding on it, and my notes on what it changed.
