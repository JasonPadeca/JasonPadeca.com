Drafted By Dice

Gatestormers

This is a story written by dice. The outcome of every major moment is determined by luck, as adjudicated by the Open Game License v1.0a. The author, such as he is, does his best to keep up with the whims of fortune.

David is an acorn: A cast-off, parentless child of the stumps, a colony of adventurers, craftsmen, and tavernmasters who make their home on the ruins of enormous trees far above a deadly forest floor.

Like all acorns, he has nothing but potential. As he takes the first steps into a classed existence as a newly minted rogue, he catches wind of a danger to the stumps far more insidious than the throngs of monsters that make their home below.

David has a chance to grow and make a name for himself in one fell swoop, but like all inhabitants of his world he is at the mercy of luck. Will the dice smile on him?
