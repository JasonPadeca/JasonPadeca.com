House rules

Unique Human

Unique Humans are versatile combatants who adapt easily to a variety of roles and occupations. They are not, however, jacks-of-all-trades and masters of none. Instead, they are specialists who often train to a high level of specific skill in their chosen path.

A unique human gains a +1 bonus to two separate stats of the player's choice, a feat of your choice, and fluency in common and one additional language.

Ranged Specialist

The ranged specialist feat grants a +2 bonus to attack rolls made with weapons with which the specialist is proficient, and +1 to weapons for which they lack a proficiency.

Goblin

Quick and resilient in a way that is sometimes compared to rats, Goblins gain +2 and +1 to their dexterity and constitution scores, respectively.

Small Target: A goblin can disengage or hide as a bonus action.

Complex Inferiority: Like a left-handed pitcher, you are much more used to fighting as a small than others are with fighting someone your height. You may force advantage on a weapon attack or disadvantage on a weapon attack against you an amount of times per long rest equal to your proficiency bonus.

Darkvision: You have darkvision up to distances of 60 feet.

Short Legs: Goblins are naturally slower than taller humanoids. They walk 25 feet per turn instead of the more standard 30.

Common

You can speak, write, read and otherwise understand the common tongue.

Orcish

You can speak, write, read and otherwise understand the language of the orcs.
