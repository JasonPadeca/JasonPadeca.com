Line numbers are reconstructed from the Globe edition milestones in the source, counting a shared verse line once — which is what makes Schmidt's own citations line up. On The Passionate Pilgrim the reconstruction disagreed with a printed anchor times out of 2,382 lines, and self-corrected at each one.

Still crude: lookup is a headword index plus suffix stripping and an irregular-verb table, no real early-modern lemmatiser. Homographs always take the first entry, so lie (recline) and lie (deceive) are not yet told apart except where a pinned citation settles it.
