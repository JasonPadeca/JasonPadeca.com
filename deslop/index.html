Generated

A friend of mine ran reactors on a fast-attack submarine. I asked him once what the actual discipline was — the thing underneath all the procedure — and he said something I’ve thought about every week since.

He said the point of reporting a two-degree anomaly isn’t the two degrees. The point is that a crew that reports two degrees at 3 a.m. on a Sunday is a crew that still has the reflex. You don’t maintain the reflex for the thing that matters. You maintain it on the things that don’t, so it’s there when something does.

We had let the reflex atrophy. Ninety seconds of atrophy, nine days deep.

Rewritten

I have a friend who once ran the reactors on a fast-attack submarine. I asked him what it was like to work in that environment, one where a small lapse in judgement could mean disaster.

He said it was all about reporting two-degree anomalies. Those, he explained, are next to nothing in the abstract. But a crew in the habit of reporting two-degree problems is proving it will catch and report real problems, too.

Surviving, he said, was a matter of building reflex via discipline. The exact same kind of discipline, I think, that both my team and I had let slip.

Questions.

Can you always beat Pangram and other detectors?

Almost. Because we rewrite every sentence and paragraph, we are in very real terms presenting detectors with 100% human text.

Good, high quality detectors (like Pangram) are very good at avoiding false positives. Other lesser products often flag human writing as AI in error; we can’t prevent that.

As a precaution against the errors we can avoid, we purchase and include a Pangram report showing 0% AI involvement with every finished piece of sufficient length — usually 50 words or more.

Why do I need to submit AI-drafted source material?

We can never be more expert than you as it relates to your field of expertise.

Conventional ghostwriting tries to bridge that gap by having you provide a rough version of what you’d like to say, but it’s lossy. Points are often misunderstood or missed entirely. Nuance is lost. More often than not, the finished work feels non-expert in a way that renders it useless.

By writing your piece with AI in advance, you can ensure it includes everything you want before we rewrite it at a world-class, human level. It ensures the best mix of your knowledge and our skill.
